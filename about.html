About Ubuntu Community Food Centre

Ubuntu Community Food Centre is a non-profit organisation that aims to support food-insecure families and individuals in South Africa.

We believe that every person deserves access to food and support. Our organisation works towards helping people experiencing food insecurity through community assistance and support programmes.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a caring community where families and individuals have access to food and opportunities for a better quality of life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide food assistance and encourage community involvement through volunteering, donations and support programmes.

Our Values

  • Ubuntu: caring and supporting one another.
  • Respect: treating everyone with dignity.
  • Community: working together to help people.
  • Compassion: understanding the needs of others.
  • Responsibility: using donations and resources responsibly.
